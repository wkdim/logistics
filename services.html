FAQ

Common Questions

How fast is same-day delivery?

Orders booked before 12:00 PM within Accra, Kumasi, and Takoradi are delivered the same day. Inter-regional transfers depend on the designated highway corridor schedule.

Are my packages insured?

Yes. Every shipment includes basic transit liability, with optional extended coverage available for high-value freight and electronics.

Can I get a custom quote for bulk freight?

Yes. Send us the details through our contact page and our freight desk will send you a volume rate.

Do you offer warehousing for small businesses?

Yes. Our flexible storage plans scale from individual pallet spaces to dedicated warehouse bays, complete with inventory management and dispatch.

Not Sure Which Service You Need?

Tell us what you are sending and we will suggest the right vehicle and route.
