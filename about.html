Our Story

From Two Vans to a Nationwide Network

Swift Logistics started in Accra in 2020, delivering for a handful of Instagram shops in Osu and East Legon with two Hyundai vans.

Today we run trucks, vans and motorbikes out of hubs in Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi and Tamale, and deliver over 5,000 parcels a month for online sellers, traders, factories and families.
